T&K Studio creates interactive game experiences, with a focus on Fortnite and Roblox.

Our work connects the places players explore with the systems they interact with. We approach environment design and gameplay development together, so the world and the rules support the same experience.

We’re a small independent team, close to both the creative decisions and the implementation. Alongside our game work, we’re exploring AI-assisted workflows to support development and iteration.

06 / AI-ASSISTED DEVELOPMENTASSISTANCE WITH ACCOUNTABILITY

More room
to explore.
The same need
for judgment.

We’re exploring how AI can support our development workflow, while keeping creative decisions, code review and playtesting in human hands.

Explore implementation options +

Use AI as a way to compare approaches, understand unfamiliar patterns and turn questions into testable implementation ideas.

Support iteration & debugging +

Investigate issues and propose focused changes. The useful output is a hypothesis to inspect and test, not an answer accepted without review.

Keep context understandable +

Explore assistance with explaining code, documenting behavior and keeping development notes clear as a prototype evolves.

Review before relying on it +

Generated code still needs to be read, tested and checked against the intended behavior and the constraints of the target platform.

These are areas of workflow exploration, not claims of a deployed AI product or a partnership with an AI provider.
