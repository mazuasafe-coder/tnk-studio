01 / THE STUDIO+

Built on curiosity.
Grounded in making.

T&K Studio is a small, independent studio focused on creating interactive digital experiences. We’re interested in how an idea feels when someone can explore it, play with it or put it to work.

Our work connects creative exploration with hands-on development: shaping concepts, building prototypes and refining the systems behind them. We keep the process close to the people doing the making.

03 / OUR APPROACH

More room for ideas.
More care in the build.

We see AI as a practical part of the development process. It helps us explore options and work through implementation, while we stay responsible for the decisions and the result.

AI-assisted. Human-directed.
  1. 01

    Start with intent

    Define the experience, the constraints and what a useful first version needs to do.

  2. 02

    Build with assistance

    Use AI to investigate approaches, support code development and shorten the distance from an idea to a prototype.

  3. 03

    Review in the real world

    Read the code, test the interactions and refine the details. A generated answer is a starting point, not a finished product.
